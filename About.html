A casual American cafe, The Family Tree Recipes Cafe was created by Brent and Terryl Scott. The idea was to have a place where family and friends can come together to enjoy great food and a great atmosphere.

When you join us for breakfast and lunch, you'll notice that...

We work hard to select only the best and freshest ingredients available. If we wouldn't serve it at our own home, we wouldn't serve it to you.

We've taken great care to create a warm and inviting atmosphere where people of all ages will feel comfortable.

We keep our restaurant and kitchen impeccably clean and well-maintained.

Our chefs are professionally trained and very talented.

Come on in and experience it for yourself! We welcome you and your family to join us for breakfast or lunch.
