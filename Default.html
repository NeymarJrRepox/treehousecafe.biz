Great American food and great atmosphere - a hidden Texas gem where everyone's welcome! With a seasonal menu and friendly staff, every trip to the Family Tree Recipes Cafe is a five star experience. It's the perfect place to come together with family & friends over breakfast and lunch.

Nestled among beautiful trees, you'll know you found the Cafe by The Hillbilly Hilton parked out front, and the treehouse in the sky with its big Cafe sign. As you walk your way up the winding stone path and wooden ramp, look for the turtles and pets living in the garden areas. And when you get to the front door, well come on in!
